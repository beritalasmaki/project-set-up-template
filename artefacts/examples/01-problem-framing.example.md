# 01 Problem framing (fictional example)

> **Fictional.** An invented example. Do not copy its content into a real project.

**Status:** reviewed · **Last updated:** 2026-03-09 · **Owner (role):** Lead designer

## The request as handed over

> "We need a new online form for building permits, with a progress bar." Requested by the service owner on 2 Mar 2026.

## The problem

Home owners applying for a building permit often do not know what the municipality needs from them, so they submit incomplete applications. Officers then have to ask for missing information, and applicants wait weeks without knowing why.

## Evidence on hand

| Finding | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Many applications are returned for missing information | Service owner, kick-off 2 Mar | opinion (no numbers given) |
| Applicants phone the office to ask for status | Case officer lead, 5 Mar | observation |

## Evidence missing

- How many applications are returned, and for what reasons. Ask the case officer lead for a sample.
- What applicants understand about the process today. No user contact so far.

## Not solving

- Replacing the case management system used by officers.
- Changing the legal requirements for a permit.

## We believe

| Hypothesis | Risk if wrong | Value if right | How we would find out |
|---|---|---|---|
| We believe applicants miss required documents because the form does not say what is needed up front. | high | high | Review a sample of returned applications |
| We believe a status page would reduce phone calls. | med | med | Ask the office to count calls for two weeks |

## What success looks like

Fewer returned applications and fewer status calls. `Baseline needed` for both before any target is set.
