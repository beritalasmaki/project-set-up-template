# 10 Decision log

**Status:** draft · **Last updated:** [date] · **Owner (role):** [role] · **Published at:** [link, or `not shared`] · **Core artefact**

One short entry per significant decision, so "why is it like this?" always has an answer. Significant means hard to reverse, affects other people, or likely to be questioned later. Decider is a role, not a personal name.

## Entry template

Copy this block for each decision.

### D-[nnn]: [Title, in one line]

- **Date:** [date]
- **Status:** proposed / accepted / superseded by D-[nnn]
- **Decided by (role):** [role]
- **Context:** [what situation forced a decision]
- **Options considered:** [option A], [option B]
- **Decision:** [what was decided]
- **Why:** [reason, with the evidence or a `gap`]
- **Gives up:** [what this costs]
- **Consequences:** [what changes because of this]
- **Revisit if:** [the signal that reopens it]
- **Related:** [feedback log IDs, artefacts, rationale]

## Done when

- [ ] Every entry has a decider role, a reason and a revisit trigger
- [ ] The reason is evidence or an honest `gap`, never invented
- [ ] The index is up to date
- [ ] Superseded decisions point to what replaced them
