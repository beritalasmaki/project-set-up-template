# Questions for finding who decides

Use these with the designer when the material does not say. Do not answer them yourself.

## By decision area

- **Scope:** who can add or remove something from what we are building?
- **Design direction:** who chooses between two designs when reviewers disagree?
- **Accessibility trade-offs:** who accepts a known fail, and on what authority?
- **Security and data handling:** who must approve what data is shown, stored or exported?
- **Legal wording:** who signs off text that has legal weight?
- **Technical feasibility:** who can say a design cannot be built as drawn?
- **Release:** who gives the final go?

## Checks

- Is the decider a role the designer can actually reach?
- Does anyone have a veto without being the decider (for example security or legal)?
- Does one role decide several areas? Is that a risk if they are unavailable?
- Did the decider change since kick-off?
- For multi-vendor work: whose decision binds the other vendors?

## Signs of an unclear decision right

- Two roles each assume the other decides.
- "We" is used in the notes without a role attached.
- A decision was reopened more than once.

Record these as gaps or risks, with the question that would resolve them.
