## 2. Understand the people

Artefacts: 02 stakeholder map, 03 requirements spec

| Skill | What it does and when it is used |
|---|---|
| `stakeholder-map` | Builds or updates the stakeholder map in artefacts/02-stakeholder-map.md from kick-off notes, meeting notes and organisation information, with roles (not personal names), interests, decision rights, conflicts set side by side, and gaps for stakeholders not yet met. Use when asked to map stakeholders, work out who decides what, prepare for a multi-stakeholder review, or after a kick-off or a change of people. Do not use for sorting review comments (that is artefacts/09-stakeholder-feedback-log.md), for user personas, or for deciding between stakeholders. |

## 4. Check

Artefacts: 05 accessibility checklist, 06 security and privacy brief, sameness review

| Skill | What it does and when it is used |
|---|---|
| `a11y-gate` | Runs a design-stage accessibility check of one screen or flow against WCAG 2.2 at the level set in project.config.md, answering each check pass, fail or cannot tell from a design, writing each fix as a design change, and listing what only code, assistive technology and disabled users can show. Uses a script for contrast and target-size maths. Use when asked to check accessibility of a design, review contrast, focus order or target size, fill in artefacts/05-accessibility-checklist.md, or before sharing or handing off a design. Do not use to claim conformance, to audit built code, or to replace testing with disabled users. |
| `sameness-check` | Reviews one finished design draft for choices left at default, listing typeface, colour, background, layout pattern, radius and shadow, iconography, motion and phrasing, marking each as chosen or inherited, and proposing up to three places for a real decision. Use after a draft exists, when a screen looks fine but could belong to any product, or before sharing a design. Do not use while generating a design, as a list of banned styles, or for accessibility checks (use a11y-gate). |
| `security-privacy-brief` | Builds the design-stage security and privacy brief in artefacts/06-security-privacy-brief.md from screens, flows and requirements, covering data items and classes, who sees what, roles and permissions, sign-in and session behaviour, consent and retention, audit needs, AI in the product, and open questions for the security and data protection owners. Use when asked about data handling, permissions or roles in a design, personal or sensitive data on screens, or before handoff of a product with several user roles. Do not use for a security review, a penetration test, legal advice or a data protection impact assessment. |

## 5. Hand off

Artefacts: 08 handoff spec, 09 stakeholder feedback log

| Skill | What it does and when it is used |
|---|---|
| `procurement-ready-handoff` | Builds the handoff spec in artefacts/08-handoff-spec.md for engineering and other vendors, stating which file and version is final, putting behaviour rules beside each screen, listing dependencies and what each vendor provides, running a ready check, and listing open questions per party. Use when designs are about to go to engineering or another supplier, when several vendors build parts of one product, or when asked for a handoff spec or ready check. Do not use for design critique, for writing contracts or procurement documents, or for work that is still being explored. |

## 6. Record and reflect

Artefacts: 10 decision log, 11 case study notes

| Skill | What it does and when it is used |
|---|---|
| `decision-log` | Adds decision records to artefacts/10-decision-log.md from meeting notes, review notes or the designer's description, with the decider as a role, the reason taken from the notes or marked as a gap, options, what was given up, a revisit trigger, and an updated index. Use right after a meeting or review where something was decided, when asked to record a decision, or when a decision is reopened, changed or superseded. Do not use for minor choices nobody will question, for sorting review comments, or to invent a reason that is not in the notes. |
| `nda-safe-case-study` | Turns project notes and decision records into portfolio case study notes in artefacts/11-case-study-notes.md that are safe to publish, with permission checked first, confidential details removed or generalised, decisions shown with options and trade-offs, outcomes taken only from real data, and an anonymisation check. Use when asked to write up a project for a portfolio, prepare a case study from client work, or check a draft for confidential details. Do not use to invent outcomes, user quotes or metrics, and do not use for projects where publication has not been approved. |
